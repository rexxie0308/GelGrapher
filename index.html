Fastest way to a figure

  1. Pick the experiment (DNA gel, RNA / Northern, Southern, SDS-PAGE, native PAGE, Western) and drop the image. Straightening, cropping, lanes, ladder and contrast are automatic. Several exposures of one gel can be dropped together; the best is chosen.
  2. Click a lane name on the figure and type; Enter jumps to the next (or paste a whole row on the left).
  3. Check the ladder card on the left and confirm it is the product you loaded. Westerns: if the ladder is not on the chemiluminescence image, load the white-light ladder image.
  4. Export… at the top right offers four things: the figure alone, a results package (figure, chart, numbers, log, methods), a complete archive (that plus the original image and the project file), and the chart alone. A pre-flight check points out problems first.

Common tasks

  • Show only some lanes: pick the wells under Lanes, or drag the sides of the red box. Names are never lost.
  • Remove a lane in the middle: select it on the image and press H (or untick its Fig. box in the lane list). A splice gap is added and the legend notes say so. The lane is still measured unless you untick Meas.
  • Western panel with target and loading control: “Strip” under Annotations, then drag across each region on the image editor. Only the strips are shown, stacked, each with its own name.
  • Measure bands: Densitometry (step 6), “+ Add band”, then click the band on the image (or drag from just above it to just below). Each lane gets a signal region and background regions above and below it, fixed on the original pixels; the value is the signed sum of signal minus background (a.u.). Normalization is optional: a reference band (it must be unchanged by the treatment and within its linear range) or the total lane signal (total-protein normalization only on a total-protein stain or stain-free image). Fold change needs a reference lane you choose. The profile, table and plot open in the analysis window under the figure; “? Needs review” marks a lane whose region or background should be checked.
  • Band sizes in every lane: the analysis window, “Band sizes”.
  • Group line: drag from one lane name to another on the figure.
  • Target band arrow: click “Target band”, then the band. The size is estimated from the ladder; values outside it are marked ≈.
  • + / − rows: add a row, then click cells on the figure to cycle + / − / empty.
  • A ladder band is wrong: drag its yellow line on the image; the others re-fit around it.
  • Wells at the bottom, or a mirrored image: the flip buttons under “Straighten and contrast”. Annotations follow.
  • Two rows of wells on one gel: the rows are found on load; switch between them under Lanes and make one figure per row. Each row keeps its names and annotations.
  • Come back to a gel later: every gel you open is kept in this browser's Library (top bar) with your latest edits; click it to continue. To take a gel to another computer, save a project file (Open / Save ▾).
  • Into a slide or notebook: Export… → Figure → Copy to clipboard.
  • More room for the image or the figure: drag the dividers between the panel, the image editor and the figure; double-click one to reset. The sizes are remembered.
  • Use it like a desktop app: on the web version, Open / Save ▾ → Install as an app (Chrome, Edge) or the browser's own “Add to Dock / Home Screen” (Safari). The installed app opens offline and, when online, fetches the newest version and offers “Reload”.

Image integrity

  • Only linear, whole-image adjustments; gamma is locked at 1 unless unlocked.
  • Camera saturation (re-image needed) and contrast clipping (fixable) are reported separately.
  • Removed lanes get a splice mark and strips a gap; never splice different gels into one panel.
  • Densitometry reads the original pixel values, never the display contrast or the resampled working image.
  • Also export the uncropped image and the original file for the supplement.

Library

Every gel you open is kept in this browser with its latest edits and reopens as you left it. The open gel is saved after every change. Click a gel to open it; click its name to rename it.

Legend and methods draft

Only text is sent to Claude: lane names, sizes, settings and numbers. The image stays in this browser. Check every statement before you use it.

Export

Figure

The figure alone, for a manuscript or slides.

Results package

One ZIP: the figure (PNG, PDF, SVG), the chart, the measurements and band sizes (CSV), the processing log and the methods text. It does not hold the original image or the project.

Complete archive

The results package plus the original image file (and ladder image) with their SHA-256, and the project file that reopens everything: for the lab archive or a reviewer.
