two minutes: where everything is

Fastest way to a figure

  1. Pick the experiment (DNA gel, RNA / Northern, Southern, SDS-PAGE, native PAGE, Western) and drop the image. Straightening, cropping, lanes, ladder and contrast are automatic. Several exposures of one gel can be dropped together; the best is chosen.
  2. Click a lane name on the figure and type; Enter jumps to the next (or paste a whole row on the left).
  3. Check the ladder on the left. Westerns: if the ladder is not on the chemiluminescence image, load the white-light marker image.
  4. “Export PNG” at the top right (or PDF, TIFF, SVG). A pre-flight check points out problems first.

Common tasks

  • Show only some lanes: pick wells under “Lanes in figure”, or drag the sides of the red box. Names are never lost.
  • Remove a lane in the middle: select it on the image and press H (or untick “Show”). A splice gap is added; state it in the legend.
  • Western panel with target and loading control: “Strip” under Annotations, then drag across each region on the raw image. Only the strips are shown, stacked, each with its own name.
  • Densitometry: step 7, “+ Band box”, click the band on the raw image. Tick the loading control (GAPDH, actin…) and every other band is divided by it in the same lane. The chart and table are in the Densitometry view next to “Figure”.
  • Band sizes in every lane: the Densitometry view, “Band sizes”.
  • Group line: drag from one lane name to another on the figure.
  • Target band arrow: click “Target band”, then the band. The size is estimated from the ladder; values outside it are marked ≈.
  • + / − rows: add a row, then click cells on the figure to cycle + / − / empty.
  • A ladder band is wrong: drag its yellow line on the image; the others re-fit around it.
  • Wells at the bottom, or a mirrored image: the flip buttons under “Straighten and contrast”. Annotations follow.
  • Two rows of wells on one gel: the rows are found on load; switch between them under “Lanes in figure” and make one figure per row. Each row keeps its names and annotations.
  • Come back to a gel later: every gel you open is kept in this browser's Library (top bar) with your latest edits; click it to continue. To take a gel to another computer, save a project file (Open / Save ▾).
  • Into a slide or notebook: Export ▾ → Copy figure to clipboard.
  • More room for the image or the figure: drag the dividers between the panel, the raw image and the figure; double-click one to reset. The sizes are remembered.
  • Use it like a desktop app: on the web version, File ▾ → Install as an app (Chrome, Edge) or the browser's own “Add to Dock / Home Screen” (Safari). The installed app opens offline and, when online, fetches the newest version and offers “Reload”.

Image integrity

  • Only linear, whole-image adjustments; gamma is locked at 1 unless unlocked.
  • Camera saturation (re-image needed) and contrast clipping (fixable) are reported separately.
  • Removed lanes get a splice mark and strips a gap; never splice different gels into one panel.
  • Densitometry uses the raw pixel values, never the display contrast.
  • Also export the uncropped image and the original file for the supplement.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo · Space+drag pan · wheel zoom · H remove/restore lane · Esc cancel
